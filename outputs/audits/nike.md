## SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- Structured search metadata, including descriptive title and header configurations.
- High-converting call-to-actions and direct booking/interaction channels.
- Strong social proof backed by visible client reviews and testimonials.

### Weaknesses
- High page load latency, which increases visitor bounce rates.
- Inconsistent social presence or missing high-value profile networks.

### Opportunities
- Compress heavy images and optimize server script load order to increase mobile performance.
- Establish a branded presence on missing social channels to capture local traffic.

### Threats
- Minimal competitive exposure.

## Actionable Recommendations
- Optimize title length (currently 25 chars, ideal is 30-60).
- Optimize site assets to lower load time (currently 4.39s).

- Place a clear, high-contrast CTA button in the hero section above the fold (e.g., 'Book a Call', 'Get Started').
- Incorporate trust badges, SSL icons, or satisfaction statements near forms to reassure prospects.
