## SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- Structured search metadata, including descriptive title and header configurations.
- High-converting call-to-actions and direct booking/interaction channels.
- Strong social proof backed by visible client reviews and testimonials.
- Broad visibility across popular social channels.

### Weaknesses
- High page load latency, which increases visitor bounce rates.

### Opportunities
- Compress heavy images and optimize server script load order to increase mobile performance.

### Threats
- Competitors with faster mobile sites capturing organic traffic due to lower bounce rates.

## Actionable Recommendations
- Optimize site assets to lower load time (currently 6.46s).
- Optimize server response time and eliminate render-blocking elements.
- Simplify contact forms by reducing fields (currently max 12 fields, aim for 3-4 fields).
- Install an interactive chat widget (e.g., HubSpot Chat, Drift) to capture visitors before they bounce.
